# 60-second demo video (the "Best 60-second demo" award, and what people actually watch)

Record the screen **and** the audio of you speaking. Phone-in-hand footage of a real person (a parent, a
relative) using it is even better than a screen recording: it proves the point.

## Before you record
- Use a made-up identity. Generate a checksum-valid fake Aadhaar: `cd backend && python -c "from app.verhoeff import make_valid_aadhaar as m; print(m('23456789012'))"`
- Quiet room, headphones on (so the app's voice doesn't feed back), Chrome, volume up.
- Do two practice runs. Re-record the take where Prisma gets everything right; don't fake it.
- Keep your credit use in mind: each full run is roughly 20 voice turns.

## Timeline
| Time | On screen | What you say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 s | Text card or you holding a thick paper form | "Millions of people can't fill a government form because reading and typing is the hard part." |
| 5-12 s | Landing page, tap **हिन्दी में शुरू करें** | "This is BoloBharo. You just talk." |
| 12-30 s | Name, date of birth, mobile number by voice. The form fills in live | Speak naturally in Hinglish. |
| 30-40 s | **The correction moment** (your best 10 seconds) | App reads the phone number back; you say "5 nahi 6" and it fixes just that digit. |
| 40-48 s | Say a **wrong Aadhaar** number | App rejects it: checksum failed. "It catches typos before they reach the form." |
| 48-56 s | Finish; stamps appear; tap **Download PDF** | |
| 56-60 s | End card | "Hindi, English, Hinglish. Built on Gnani Prisma and Timbre. #GnaniAI" |

## Tips
- Subtitles on. Most people watch muted at first.
- Lead with the human problem, not the tech. The tech shows up in the correction and checksum moments.
- First frame matters on LinkedIn: no logo animation, start with the problem.
- Post natively (upload the video to LinkedIn/X), don't paste a YouTube link.
